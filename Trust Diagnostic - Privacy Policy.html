Whenever we process your Personal Information under the 'legitimate interest' lawful basis we make sure that we take into account your rights and interests and will not process your personal information if we feel that there is an imbalance.

Your Rights Over Your Personal Data

Under data protection law, you have several rights regarding your personal data. Before we respond, we may need proof of your identity and sometimes extra details to help us locate your data. We aim to respond within one month of receiving your request (and any proof needed). If your request is complex or you make multiple requests, we may take up to three months, but we will let you know if that is the case.

1. Right to Access
You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. This is called a "subject access request." Requests must be made in writing to: info@imaginalventures.com, or by post to Data Protection Officer, 53 Melwood Avenue, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada, B3N 1E4.

2. Right to Object
You can object to us processing your personal data when we rely on "legitimate interests" as our legal basis. If we have strong legal reasons to continue, we may still process your data. Otherwise, we will stop. You can exercise this right by emailing info@imaginalventures.com. You can also withdraw consent for marketing at any time by unsubscribing.

3. Right to Stop Marketing
You can ask us at any time not to send you marketing or advertising.

4. Right to Correct Data
If the data we hold about you is inaccurate, you can ask us to correct it. Once we verify accuracy, we will update it promptly.

5. Right to Erasure
You can ask us to delete your data in certain situations, such as where we no longer need it or we processed it unlawfully. If deletion is required by law, we will either delete or fully anonymise the data so it cannot identify you.

6. Right to Restrict Processing
You can ask us to limit how we use your data in some cases, for example while we check its accuracy, or when you need us to keep it so you can use it for legal claims. When processing is restricted, we will only store your data and will not use it further unless you consent, or we need it for legal reasons or to protect others.

Additional rights include: the right to withdraw consent at any time; the right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data; the right to portability, so you or a third party can receive your data in a structured, commonly used, electronic form; and the right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not currently carry out any automated decision-making.

Please note, some of these rights only apply in certain circumstances and we may not be able to fulfil every request.

Cookies

A 'cookie' is a name for a small file, usually of letters and numbers, which is downloaded onto your device, like your computer, mobile phone or tablet when you visit a website. They let websites recognise your device, so that the sites can work more effectively, and also gather information about how you use the site. A cookie, by itself, can't be used to identify you.

We use cookies to distinguish you from other users of our website. This helps us to provide you with a good experience when you come to our website and also allows us to improve the user experience. We use all four categories of cookies:

Strictly necessary cookies are essential for you to move around our website and to use its features, like our cart and your account.

Performance cookies collect anonymous information about how you use our site, like which pages are visited most.

Functionality cookies collect anonymous information that remember choices you make to improve your experience, like your text size or location. They may also be used to provide services you have asked for such as watching a video or commenting on a blog.

Targeting or advertising cookies collect information about your browsing habits in order to make advertising relevant to you and your interests.

You can opt out of all our cookies (except the strictly necessary ones) via your browser settings. But, if you choose to refuse all cookies, our website may not function for you as we would like it to.
